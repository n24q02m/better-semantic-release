## 2024-05-24 - [Uncompiled Regexes in LRU Cached Function Loop]
**Learning:** In `src/semantic_release/helpers.py`, `parse_git_url` was recompiling regular expressions dynamically using `re.compile(pattern).sub(...)` inside a loop every time the function was called. Even though `parse_git_url` is memoized with `@lru_cache`, cache eviction or lots of varying unique URLs causes it to re-execute frequently. Python's `re.compile()` inside a hot loop is an anti-pattern. While `re.sub()` internally caches up to 512 patterns, compiling them explicitly once at module scope avoids the internal LRU lookup cost of `re` for a small but consistent performance win.
**Action:** Extract static regex strings from within tight functions, and compile them globally at module level into variables, applying the compiled objects (`pattern.sub()`) in loops.
